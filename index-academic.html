About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, supervised by Prof. Michael R. Lyu. My research interests include high-performance computing, parallel computing, AI systems, and LLM inference optimization.

Prior to joining CUHK, I received my B.Eng. degree in Computer Science. I have participated in multiple supercomputing competitions including ASC Student Supercomputer Challenge and Student Cluster Competition (SCC), where I gained extensive experience in HPC system optimization and parallel algorithm design.
