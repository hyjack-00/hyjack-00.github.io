About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Michael R. Lyu. My research focuses on high-performance computing, parallel computing, and AI systems, with particular emphasis on optimizing large language model inference and memory-compute pipeline design.

Prior to joining CUHK, I received my B.Eng. degree in Computer Science from Sun Yat-sen University in 2023. During my undergraduate studies, I was an active member of the Sun Yat-sen University Supercomputing Team, where I participated in multiple international competitions including the ASC Student Supercomputer Challenge and Student Cluster Competition (SCC). Through these experiences, I developed expertise in HPC system optimization, parallel algorithm design, and performance tuning on heterogeneous architectures.

Efficient Parallel Computing Strategies for Large Language Model Inference
Yujie Huang, Co-author A, Co-author B, Michael R. Lyu
In Preparation
We propose novel parallel computing strategies to optimize large language model inference, achieving significant speedup through memory-compute pipeline optimization and efficient workload distribution across heterogeneous computing resources. Our approach addresses the challenges of memory bandwidth bottlenecks and computational inefficiencies in modern GPU clusters.
In Prep
Optimizing Memory-Compute Pipeline in High-Performance Computing Applications
Co-author X, Yujie Huang, Michael R. Lyu
Target Conference, 2025
This work investigates memory-compute pipeline optimization techniques in HPC applications, with particular focus on RDMA-based communication patterns and dataflow analysis. We demonstrate improved computational efficiency on modern heterogeneous architectures through systematic pipeline design and performance modeling.
